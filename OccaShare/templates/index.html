{# ======= 6. ABOUT US ======= #}

About OccaServe

OccaServe is a platform that helps independent caterers showcase their food and services, connect with customers, and manage bookings and day-to-day operations more efficiently.

Customers can use OccaServe to discover catering providers, compare their offerings, and coordinate bookings. The caterers themselves prepare and provide the food and services customers choose.

Our goal is to make it easier for caterers to run their operations and for customers to find the right catering service for their occasion.
