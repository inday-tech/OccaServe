Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains what information OccaServe handles and how it is used when you use the platform.

OccaServe ("we," "our," or "us") is a technology platform that helps customers connect with independent caterers. This policy describes the information handled through OccaServe and how it is used to operate the platform. Caterers are independent service providers and may separately handle information they receive to fulfill a booking.
